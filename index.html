▚ HOW IT WORKS ▞

  1. 1

    SKIM THE RULES

    Start by reading our submission rules so you know exactly what we do and don't allow before you pitch.

  2. 2

    RUN THE CHECKLIST

    Tick off the requirements to confirm your game's a fit. Once everything's checked, you're ready to submit.

  3. 3

    SUBMIT YOUR GAME

    Fill out a quick form: what stage of development you're in, what your game is about, and a few other details.

  4. 4

    WE REVIEW — APPROVE OR DENY

    Our team reviews every submission and gets back to you with a clear yes or no.

  5. 5

    WE REACH OUT

    If it's a yes, we connect to talk details. Need confidentiality first? We can sign an NDA whenever it's necessary.

  6. 6

    SIGN THE AGREEMENT

    We send over the Marketing & Publishing Agreement. Sign it, and we get to work launching your game.

THE DEAL AT A GLANCE

  • 70 / 30 You keep 70% of net profit — profit after platform cuts (like Steam's), not gross revenue. We take 30%.
  • RECOUP If we fund your game, we recoup that investment from profits before the split kicks in.
  • MILESTONES A termination & abandonment clause lets us recover our funding if a funded project misses its milestones or gets abandoned.
  • NDA Confidentiality agreements are available any time you need one.

This is a plain-English summary — the signed Marketing & Publishing Agreement contains the full terms.
