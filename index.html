  • RECOUP If we fund your game, we recoup that investment from profits before the split kicks in.
  • MILESTONES A termination & abandonment clause lets us recover our funding if a funded project misses its milestones or gets abandoned.
  • NDA Confidentiality agreements are available any time you need one.

▚ FAQ ▞

What kinds of games do you publish?

Original indie games with a clear hook — any genre. We focus on PC/Steam and itch.io, and we're open to console ports where it makes sense.

Does it cost anything to pitch?

Nope. Pitching is completely free, and we read every submission that comes in.

What's the revenue split?

70/30 in your favor, calculated on net profit — that's after platform cuts (like Steam's 30%), not gross revenue.

Do you fund development?

Sometimes — it's case by case. If we do fund your game, we recoup that investment from profits before the split kicks in.

Do I keep the rights to my game?

Yes. You keep ownership of your game and its IP. We handle publishing and marketing under the agreement — we don't take your game from you.

How long does review take?

We aim to get back to you within a couple of weeks. Busy stretches can take a little longer, but you'll always hear from us.

Can we sign an NDA first?

Absolutely. Flag it on your submission and we'll sort out a confidentiality agreement before you share anything sensitive.
