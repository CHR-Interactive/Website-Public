PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle the information you share with us.

1. Data Collection: CHR Interactive Inc. only collects personal identifier information that you explicitly choose to provide through our digital pitch and contact forms. This data includes your name, studio name, email address, and submitted game details.

2. Data Purpose and Usage: Your data is used exclusively by CHR Interactive Inc. to evaluate your video game submission and communicate development opportunities directly with you. We do not use automated profiling algorithms to reject pitches.

3. Confidentiality and Sharing: We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information or proprietary game concepts to any third parties. All submissions are treated as strictly confidential business assets within CHR Interactive Inc.

4. Jurisdiction and Legal Compliance: This Privacy Policy is governed under the laws of the Province of Alberta, Canada, and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). Data is securely processed on local devices and secure cloud environments.

5. Your Rights & Data Removal: You retain the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your data from our systems. For data removal requests or privacy inquiries, contact our data compliance officer directly at legal@chrinteractive.com.
