PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle the information you share with us.

CHR Interactive values your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data collected via chrinteractive.com:

  1. Data Collection: We only collect information that you explicitly choose to provide through our contact and pitch forms (such as your name, studio name, email address, and game details).
  2. Data Usage: Your data is used exclusively by the CHR Interactive team to evaluate your game submission and communicate directly with you.
  3. Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or proprietary game concepts to any third parties. All submissions are kept strictly confidential within our core team.
  4. Contact: For data removal requests, contact us directly at legal@chrinteractive.com.
