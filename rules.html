SUBMISSION RULES

Give this a skim before you pitch. It covers what we're looking for, what we can't accept, and the terms you agree to when you submit.

✅ WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

  • Original games with a clear hook — something that stands out.
  • A playable build, vertical slice, or a strong prototype. A trailer or demo helps a lot.
  • Developers who fully own their game and its assets.
  • Projects targeting PC/Steam — though we're open to other platforms too.
  • Teams (or solo devs) who are responsive and easy to work with.

🚫 WHAT WE DON'T ACCEPT

  • Games using stolen, pirated, or unlicensed assets you don't have the rights to.
  • Clones, asset-flips, or games that copy another title's identity.
  • Content that is illegal, hateful, or infringes someone else's intellectual property.
  • Projects with unresolved ownership disputes between team members.
  • Submissions that misrepresent the game's current state or your rights to it.

📋 BEFORE YOU SUBMIT — CHECKLIST

Make sure you can honestly tick every box below before filling out the form:

  • I own (or have properly licensed) all IP, art, code, and music in my game.
  • My game has a playable build, vertical slice, or clear prototype.
  • I've gathered any links I have — trailer, demo, store page, or press kit.
  • My game doesn't infringe on anyone else's intellectual property.
  • I've read and agree to the Submission Terms & Conditions below.

By submitting your game prototype, trailer, or pitch deck to CHR Interactive, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms:

  1. No Obligation: Submitting your game does not create a business partnership, contract, or agreement. CHR Interactive is under no obligation to review, respond to, or sign your project.
  2. Independent Development: You understand that CHR Interactive receives many pitches. You agree that you will not be entitled to any compensation if CHR Interactive publishes a game that contains similar ideas, themes, or mechanics to your submission.
  3. Intellectual Property: You warrant that you are the sole owner of all intellectual property, art, code, and music within the submitted build, and that your submission does not infringe on any third-party copyrights.
